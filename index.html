Darker blue water is deeper. Arrows point the way the water is moving; bigger and warmer-coloured arrows mean bigger swell or a faster current. Slide or press Play to see the next 48 hours. Depth map © Esri, GEBCO, NOAA.

Surf and tide data (tides are shown at the top with the forecast) from Open-Meteo marine forecasts, updated every 30 minutes. Wave models are less precise right at the shore, and tide times can be out by up to half an hour. For boating, check official Bureau of Meteorology tide tables.
